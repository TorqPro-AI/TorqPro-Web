VERIFIED PRODUCT SCOPE · SOURCE v3.4.0

Engineering capability, with boundaries.

The application repository documents deterministic torque recommendations and advanced VDI 2230 Blatt 1 joint calculations. The following distinctions matter in critical applications.

01 / STRESS

Axial stress scope

Reported axial operating stress does not include tightening torsion and is not the full reduced-stress check.

02 / CONTACT

Average contact pressure

The reported pressure is a scalar average, not a radial pressure distribution or finite-element result.

03 / AI

Deterministic authority

AI provides explanations and context. It does not change engineering calculation outputs.
